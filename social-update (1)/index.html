FAQ

Good questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

How soon will we see results?

Most campaigns start delivering reach and engagement within 1–2 weeks of creator content going live. Full results, including saves, shares and conversion signals, are reported at the 4-week and 8-week marks.

Do you only work in Hindi and English?

No. Our creator bench covers Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali and Gujarati, depending on where your campaign runs.

Do you work with brands outside beauty and D2C?

Our core strength is beauty, D2C and FMCG, but the same casting and campaign model works for any category that benefits from real-household trust, including home care, food and lifestyle.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram is our primary platform, supported by YouTube Shorts and meme and community page distribution. We don't run TikTok campaigns, since the platform isn't available in India.

Do we need to provide creators?

No. We handle sourcing, vetting, outreach, negotiation, briefing and management. You approve the shortlist and the content; we run the rest.

Can we start with a smaller pilot?

Yes. We regularly scope single-city or single-region pilots before scaling into a full Pan-India rollout.
